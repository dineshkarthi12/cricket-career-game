# 🏏 FULL CRICKET CAREER MODE
## INITIAL → INTERNATIONAL PLAYER

---

## STAGE 1 — CRICKET BEGINNER
Age: 8–12

↓ School Cricket
↓ Local Cricket Academy
↓ Local Club Matches
↓ Basic Training
↓ Batting / Bowling / Fielding Development
↓ Fitness Development
↓ School / Local Tournaments

Possible outcomes:
- Good performance → District trials
- Average performance → Continue local level
- Poor performance → Training + comeback
- Injury → Recovery + rehabilitation

## 🎮 IMPORTANT GAME RULE

**NO AUTOMATIC PROMOTION.**

Every stage depends on:
- Performance
- Form
- Fitness
- Age
- Competition
- Selection
- Team requirements
- Match conditions
- Injuries
- Opportunities
- Consistency

A player can:
- WIN → PROMOTE
- LOSE → STAY
- PERFORM BADLY → BENCH
- GET INJURED → RECOVER
- GET DROPPED → COMEBACK
- PERFORM EXCEPTIONALLY → FAST-TRACK

But no guaranteed superstar path.
